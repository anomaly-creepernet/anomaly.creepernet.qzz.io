published 2026-08-06

Not All Free Domains Are Equal

Getting a free domain is easy. Getting a free domain that Google will actually index, that can send email to Gmail without landing in spam, and that will not disappear tomorrow, is much harder. This is a rundown of what actually works as of 2026 based on real usage, not just what the providers claim.

The Burned TLDs

.tk, .ml, .ga, .cf, .gq (Freenom) are effectively dead for any serious use. Freenom's free registration program ended, and even before that, the reputation damage was permanent. Gmail and ProtonMail routinely reject or spam-filter mail from these TLDs. Google Search treats sites on these domains as very low priority for indexing. If you have one of these domains, you are fighting an uphill battle against years of spam abuse by millions of users.

What Works: .cyou and .bond

Nicnames (an ICANN-accredited registrar, IANA ID 4156) offers one free .cyou and one free .bond domain per person. As of 2026, both TLDs have clean reputations. Email from these domains is delivered normally to Gmail and Proton inboxes. Google Search indexes sites on these domains without issues. The .cyou domain is operated by shortdot SA (the same company behind .icu), and CentralNic runs the .bond registry backend.

The free registration is legitimate — nicnames is a real registrar that also sells premium domains. The free domains are a customer acquisition strategy. The domains are yours, you get full DNS control, and there are no hidden fees or forced ads.

Register your free domains at nicnames.com or nicnames.net. You get one .cyou and one .bond. Choose wisely because there is no way to get more without paying.

What Works: GitHub Pages Subdomains

If you have a custom domain on Cloudflare, you can create unlimited subdomains and point them to separate GitHub Pages repositories. Each subdomain is treated as a separate site by Google. anomaly.cnetai.cyou and blog.cnetai.cyou can be completely different sites with their own SEO profiles. This is essentially free hosting with indexable domains, as long as you have the parent domain.

The NS Delegation Trap

Some free hosting services (like MyOwnFreeHost) require you to change your domain's nameservers to theirs. This works but it means you lose all DNS control. You cannot use Cloudflare, you cannot set up custom email elsewhere, and you cannot easily move to another host later. The NS lock-in is real and it is the main reason to avoid these services for anything you care about. If a hosting provider needs NS delegation, it is because they use dynamic IPs and need to update DNS records on their end. There is no workaround for the zone apex without NS delegation (see RFC 1912). Subdomains via CNAME work, but the root domain is locked.

SEO Checklist for Free Domains

  • Unique, non-duplicate content on every page
  • Proper meta descriptions and title tags
  • robots.txt that allows crawling
  • sitemap.xml submitted to Google Search Console
  • OG tags for social sharing
  • HTTPS (automatic with Cloudflare or GitHub Pages)
  • Fast load times (static sites on Cloudflare CDN score well)
  • Internal linking between your pages and subdomains
